# AirPaste v1.0.0 Beta 4

Version `1.0.0-beta.4` · Windows · November 2026

## Listing

**Title:** AirPaste

**Subtitle:** Your desktop canvas for everything you save

**Short description (≤ 80 chars):** Paste links, images, files and notes onto one infinite, local-first canvas.

**Description:**
AirPaste is a local-first desktop canvas for everything you save. Paste links, images, files and notes, then arrange them freely on an infinite board. Group them, stick notes on them, tie them together and flip between pages. Your canvases stay as plain files on your computer: private, offline and yours.

**Keywords:** AirPaste, infinite canvas, visual bookmarks, link saver, moodboard, whiteboard, sticky notes, research board, inspiration board, spatial notes, local-first, offline, desktop app, productivity

## What's in this beta

- **Drawing tools.** Line, Ink pen and Highlighter in the dock, with colours and sizes. Strokes are saved with the canvas and work with undo.
- **3D stacks.** Select tiles and press G: they fly into a 3D pile you can move as one. Double-click or Shift+G to unstack.
- **Curved view.** A toggle next to Home bends the canvas like a shallow dome, with a soft blurred rim.
- **Files view on Home.** A third view next to List and Cards: every canvas is a folder holding its real tiles, and folders spread their canvases out when opened.
- **Pixel trail.** A small trail of fading pixels follows the pointer over the grey window frame.
- **Cleaner top bar.** Borderless buttons. Home opens in Cards view.
- **New installer.** A minimal, Apple-style setup window.
- **Copy image.** Right-click a link tile → Copy image puts its full-size picture on the clipboard.
- **Double-click to type.** Double-clicking empty canvas starts a quick text (it used to make a sticky).
- **Paste lands in the middle.** Copied or cut tiles, links and images paste at the centre of the view, also across files.
- **Pages panel in the frame.** The pages list is a cut-out of the window frame, identical on both sides of split screen.
- **Quick checklist.** Grows with its tasks, scrolls past a height, and the add box stays pinned at the bottom.
- **Grow-pop animations.** Tiles pop in when created or first shown; big canvases show a placeholder for each tile while they load.
- **Top bar.** Home, Curved view and Theme are matching buttons side by side; curved view is remembered per page and per split pane.
- **Fixes.** Smooth when scrolling and zooming at once; closing a tab with many tiles is instant; no more "changed elsewhere" save prompts; no focus border around text boxes; curved view no longer bends huge tiles when zoomed far in.

## Known limitations

- Beta software: keep backups of canvases you care about.
- Windows only for this beta.
- The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may warn on first run ("More info" → "Run anyway").

## Install

Run `AirPaste Setup 1.0.0-beta.4.exe`. It installs to `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Programs\AirPaste`. Uninstall from Windows Settings → Apps, or Start → Uninstall AirPaste. Your canvases are kept.
