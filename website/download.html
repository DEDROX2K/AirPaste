New in Beta 4

Draw, pile, bend and browse your canvases in new ways.

Drawing tools

A line, an ink pen and a highlighter in the dock, with colours and sizes. Sketch and mark up right on the canvas.

3D stacks

Select tiles and press G: they fly into a real pile you can carry around as one. Double-click to spread them out again.

Curved view

One toggle bends the whole board like a shallow dome, with a soft blur at the rim.

Files view

A new way to browse Home: every canvas is a folder holding its real tiles, and folders spread their canvases out when you open them.

Pixel trail

A little trail of pixels follows your pointer around the window frame.

Cleaner everywhere

Borderless top bar, Home opens in Cards view, and a calm new installer.

Copy image

Right-click a link tile and copy its picture, full size, to paste into any other app.

Double-click to type

Double-click anywhere on the canvas and start typing. Pasted tiles land right in the middle of your view.

Pages in the frame

The pages list is now part of the window frame, the same on both sides of split screen.

Quicker checklist

The quick checklist grows as you add tasks, scrolls when it's long, and keeps its add box pinned at the bottom.

Snappier

Tiles pop in as they appear, big canvases show where every tile is while they load, and tabs close instantly.

Theme up top

Home, curved view and theme sit together in the top bar, and curved view is remembered per page.
